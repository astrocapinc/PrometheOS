# Code of conduct

PrometheOS is a research tool, and its community should feel like a good lab: people disagree about ideas, help each other and treat each other with respect.

## Expected behaviour

- Be welcoming to people of every background, career stage and level of experience.
- Critique work and ideas, not people. Assume good faith, and ask before you assume.
- Respect privacy. Never post someone else's personal data, unpublished research or recordings.
- Accept a maintainer's decision on scope gracefully, even when you disagree.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or language, sexual attention, threats, deliberate intimidation, publishing others' private information, and sustained disruption of discussions are not acceptable in any project space: issues, pull requests, discussions and any other channel run for the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report a concern privately to the maintainer, through the contact in the maintainer's GitHub profile. Reports are kept confidential. The maintainer may edit or remove contributions, and warn, suspend or ban a participant, in proportion to what happened. Someone who is the subject of a report does not handle it.

This code applies in all project spaces, and when someone represents the project elsewhere. It is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1, but is a shorter text of its own.
